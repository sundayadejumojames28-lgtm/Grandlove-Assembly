Who We Are

About Grandlove Assembly

A Bible-centred Christian ministry focused on the love of Christ, sound biblical teaching, discipleship, prayer and fellowship.

Our Story

Built on the Word, driven by love

Grandlove Assembly exists to propagate the love of Christ and to raise people who know God, love one another and fulfil their God-given purpose. Our motto, “God Is Love” (1 John 4:8), shapes everything we do.

The Holy Bible is our foundation and guide. We believe it is the inspired Word of God, sufficient for faith and life, and we are committed to teaching it soundly and living it faithfully.

Our Vision

To raise a people who reflect the love and light of Christ, grounded in God's Word and fulfilling their God-given purpose in every sphere of life.

Our Mission

To propagate the love of Christ, teach the Bible soundly, make disciples, build believers through prayer and fellowship, and empower young people through spiritual growth and education.

Statement of Faith

What We Believe

  • The Holy Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our foundation for faith and conduct.
  • There is one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus Christ is the Son of God, who died for our sins and rose again.
  • Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.
  • The Holy Spirit empowers believers to live a holy and fruitful life.
  • God is love, and believers are called to love God and one another.
