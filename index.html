Welcome

A family built on the love of Christ

Grandlove Assembly is committed to propagating the love of Christ, teaching God's Word, building believers, and helping young people fulfil their God-given purpose.

Whether you are new to faith or have walked with the Lord for years, you are welcome here. Come and worship with us, grow in the Scriptures, and shine as a light in your world.
