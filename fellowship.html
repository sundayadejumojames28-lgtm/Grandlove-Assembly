Grandlove Assembly Students Fellowship

A university-based Christian fellowship for students of Osun State University (UNIOSUN), Osogbo Campus.

Our Fellowship

Christ-centred life on campus

University life brings new opportunities and new pressures. The Grandlove Assembly Students Fellowship is a home for UNIOSUN students who want to keep Christ at the centre while pursuing their academic goals.

Together we study God's Word, worship, pray, and support one another to grow spiritually and excel academically.
